HOW TO PLAY

Last tank standing wins the round. Aim your barrel, set your power, pick a weapon, and fire. Shells arc under gravity and drift with the wind — watch the WIND readout in the HUD and adjust. Terrain is fully destructible: craters open up, dirt settles, and tanks fall if the ground is blasted out from under them (falling hurts unless you have a parachute).

Between rounds, spend your winnings in the shop on bigger weapons and defensive gear. Cash earns 5% interest each round, and prizes are paid for damage dealt, kills, and survival. The player with the most round wins at the end of the tournament is champion.

THE ARMORY

Baby Missile is free and unlimited. Everything else is bought in the shop.

DEFENSIVE GEAR

Batteries also power driving. With a battery active, each turn you can either heal or spend the charge driving your tank (A/D, up to 30 pixels). Drive off a cliff and you'll fall — bring a parachute. Shields auto-deploy at round start (strongest first) and absorb damage until depleted.

In 3-4 player games, AIs gang up on whoever is weakest. Don't be weakest.

TIPS

Fire a Tracer first to learn the wind. Dirt weapons are underrated — burying an opponent is as good as hitting them. Rollers follow the terrain downhill, so shoot uphill of a tank in a valley. And the Reaper's skull means exactly what you think it means.
